# Origin War: systemic survival horror

Survive a lonely, failing station through observation, preparation and consequential movement. The current 0.2 development candidate replaces the earlier RPG design under the [owner-approved authority](docs/directives/SURVIVAL_HORROR_20261021.md).

The ten-deck, seeded descent has no XP, levels, attributes or living human NPCs. A twelve-bulk physical pack, finite oxygen and power, mechanical light and sound, remembered machine observations, local circuits and doors determine your options. ALICE is the station-resident AI and the only live conversational presence. Her authored offline exchanges follow actual circumstances; the ending depends on physical operations rather than completing dialogue.

The illustrated presentation uses named variable-size artwork, foot anchors, depth sorting, painted motion and dependable suit instrumentation. Original masters, editable sources, exact voice text and asset provenance are retained. The current candidate is **not artistically accepted for commercial release**. Human difficulty,90–120-minute pacing, extended listening and owner artistic approval remain separate gates. See [survival qualification](docs/SURVIVAL_QUALIFICATION.md), [manual](MANUAL.md), [progress](docs/PROGRESS.md) and [ledger](docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

Build with JDK 21: `gradlew.bat test verifyAssets`. Launch with `gradlew.bat :lwjgl3:run`. The Windows app includes its runtime; see [distribution](tools/distribution/README.md). Survival profiles use `%LOCALAPPDATA%/OriginWar/survival-0.2`, with no legacy-save migration. Earlier source, reference material and release deliveries remain preserved; historical verification does not certify the redesign.
