# Origin War art comparison

Open **CompareArt.cmd** for the staged room. **F6 switches painted/rendered art immediately**, preserving the same position, lighting, resources and simulation. The banner identifies the current method and unavailable artwork. ESC pauses; F12 saves a native screenshot.

WASD / arrows move. Alt + arrow turns the body and helmet light. Ctrl + arrows aim freely; Backspace clears aim. F fires a real shot. L changes the real helmet lamp. Tab opens the physical pack; use its floor-item rows to equip the rifle and heavy shell underfoot. Equipping consumes actual time. Changing appearance with F6 is free. The far room contains a live machine; ammunition, oxygen and damage remain real.

This is authored test geometry in the production engine. It does not establish campaign generation quality, difficulty or pacing. Its profile is isolated under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/OriginWar/art-comparison-20261021/art-comparison-lab`. Each launcher begins a new isolated test run; the normal game profile is preserved.

**CampaignArtPreview.cmd** starts a separate ordinary seed-zero campaign with the new environment and rendered officer. F6 compares the painted candidate there too. It uses its own profile.

The candidates have different coverage and finish limitations. Missing requested equipment/actions are labeled UNAVAILABLE instead of showing a false weapon or armor. Consult the included art proof documents and coverage report. These are review candidates; owner artistic selection and the 15–20-minute quality slice remain open.

Please compare normal-speed foot placement, stops, anatomy, weapon grips, weight, illustrated finish and readability under the same light. Source-frame counts and technical validation do not establish artistic quality.
