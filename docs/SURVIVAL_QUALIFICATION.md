## Implemented behavior

The authoritative engine now uses statless capabilities, physical item identities and twelve bulk, conserved partial transfers, bounded cells and finite seed-fixed supplies. Free inspection and forecasts do not consume simulation randomness. Paid facing/lamp changes, final-tick work, mechanical light and topology-based sound determine movement risk. Machines retain saved patrol/investigation/pursuit/search/return information and explicit door limits. Local circuits and optional braces/probes change actual access. The Constructor fabricates finite stock through powered machinery.

All ten decks use physical releases and public routes. Records and service charts preserve acquired evidence without reading gates. ALICE has no actor/follower; other people exist through stored material. Command authentication, transmission hardware and pod occupancy govern the refusal/broadcast/sacrifice/Mars escape. Authored speech completion is independent of progression.

Fresh per-expedition version2 saves use a process lease, durable activation pointer, same-run backup/death record and one background writer. Failed writes hold world input and retry the immutable committed outcome. Malformed settings recover defaults; malformed knowledge arrays are rejected before copying so valid backups can recover.
