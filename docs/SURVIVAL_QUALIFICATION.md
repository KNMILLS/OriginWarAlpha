# Survival redesign qualification

The 0.2 development candidate implements the owner-approved 21 October 2026 survival replacement across all ten decks. It is playable and technically tested. Commercial release remains open pending the illustrated slice, unfamiliar-player evaluation, pacing, extended listening and owner artistic acceptance. Earlier 0.1 records certify only the preserved RPG release.

## Implemented behavior

Capabilities are statless. Twelve physical bulk, individual item identities, partial transfers, bounded cells and finite seed-fixed supplies replace character growth. Free inspection and forecasts consume neither time nor simulation randomness. Paid movement, facing, lamp changes and final-tick work make commitments consequential. Mechanical illumination and topology-based sound govern player observations and machine information. Machines save patrol, investigation, pursuit, search and return memory, with explicit door limits and bounded searches of unreachable locations. Local circuits and optional braces/probes change actual access. The Constructor fabricates finite stock through its powered machinery.

Every deck has a physical public release and descent route. Service charts preserve static geometry without revealing current hidden objects or overwriting newer observations. ALICE has no station actor or follower. Other people exist through archives and traces. Command authentication, transmission hardware and pod occupancy govern refusal, broadcast, sacrifice and the Mars escape. Reading and speech completion never unlock a mechanism.

Version-two profiles use a fresh namespace, process lease, per-expedition files, durable activation pointer, same-run backup/death record and one background writer. Failed writes hold world input and retry the captured outcome. Malformed settings recover safely; incomplete or malformed saved fields are rejected before copying, allowing a valid same-run backup to recover.

## Reproduction

Build with JDK 21 and the wrapper. `ORIGINWAR_GENERATION_SEEDS=10000` selects the required seed sweep and is a tracked test input. `gradlew.bat test verifyAssets` checks rules, persistence, media bindings and notices. Exact native streams are documented in [the handoff](../tools/testing/survival/NATIVE_HANDOFF.md).

Specialist briefs: [art](design/art/SURVIVAL_PRODUCTION.md), [animation](design/art/SURVIVAL_ANIMATION.md), [sound and voice](design/sound/SURVIVAL_PRODUCTION.md). Exporters live under `tools/media/survival_art`, `survival_animation`, `survival_equipment_motion` and `survival_sound`. The historical pixel packer requires an explicit historical-rebuild switch. Rendering, audio and dialogue playback cannot advance the world or simulation RNG.
