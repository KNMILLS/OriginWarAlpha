## Reproduction and limits

Use pinned official portable Blender4.5.3 LTS with `--background --factory-startup --python tools/media/officer_production/build.py`, then run `python tools/media/officer_production/review.py` and `python tools/media/officer_production/review_v2.py --full`. Default rendering is75%; `--master` reproduces256px reference. `--look-only` exports four idles; `--body-look` and `--body-motion` reproduce bounded V3 pilots in their review directories. Source retains weighted geometry, material nodes, camera and a keyframed walking action. Other authored pose rules are editable Python source, not hand-painted projects. The old exporter is guarded reference-only.

Anatomical foundations are Dan Ulrich's **Head (Animation) - Realistic** and **Body Male - Realistic** from official Blender Human Base Meshes1.4.1, both explicitly CC0 on their actual collections. Production imports the head/four eye meshes and one body mesh; the body's head/hands are removed from its derived garment copy. The immutable clean body library retains its three original meshes. Unrelated Rain Rig/CC-BY content is not imported. Exact official URLs, hashes, declarations and extraction evidence are under `source/anatomy/`. The garment is derived CC0 topology; its fitting/envelope/reinforcement design, original helmet/gear/rig, pose rules, materials and lighting are project work. No purchase was made.

Other weapons/armor, broader acting/illustration polish and campaign expansion remain out of scope. Root owns native integration and the owner owns artistic acceptance. Technical checks, source previews, native gameplay, human reaction and commercial finish remain distinct.
