# Origin War benchmark specialist briefs

These are two bounded requests for fixed-price proposals for the first finished playable room. They are ready to accompany the benchmark build, actual before/after footage, reference reel and remaining-defect assessment. No supplier, fee, commercial license or purchase is assumed. Quotes and commercial commitments require owner approval.

## Senior look development and environment assignment

Deliver one pale-suited human officer and one receiving/inspection workplace that read as the same finished illustration in the supplied Windows game. Use the actual 720p and 1080p camera, lighting and sprite scale, with the helmet lamp on and off. Preserve human proportions, vulnerable industrial identity, substantial machinery, restrained tension, existing equipment identities and collision. The brief is the remaining visible gap recorded in the benchmark assessment, rather than an unrestricted redesign.

The result includes improved textile/shell/seal/hardware separation, garment construction, controlled highlights and purposeful wear; one coherent inspection equipment group with grounded shadows; and a side-on door whose passage, obstruction and operating states read immediately. Integrate usable exports in the production engine. A concept painting alone is not the deliverable.

Supply editable scene/material/texture sources, all dependencies and licenses, fixed camera/origin settings, reproducible RGBA exports and an in-game before/after comparison. Retain the existing 192 MiB active art allocation and agree exact loadout/room texture residency with the integrator. Include a source and commercial-rights inventory identifying third-party material and any attribution obligation.

Ask the proposal to price a first integrated look check and two defined revision rounds, state what each round includes, identify additional-round pricing, and specify final acceptance against the supplied scene at both resolutions and lamp states. Final files must remain editable without a continuing vendor service.

## Character animation polish assignment

Deliver the continuous normal-speed sequence idle → start → repeated walking → corner → stop → ready → aim → fire → recovery in all four directions, plus unarmed movement and one rifle/heavy-armor sample. Resolve the demonstrated acting defects in the supplied in-game footage. Establish contact, compression, passing and push-off poses before intermediate frames; preserve believable weight, pelvis/torso motion, head stability, foot roll, grip and equipment attachment.

The supplied engine owns authoritative grid movement, tactical time, collision and combat outcomes. Animation and bounded transition handling adapt to that contract. Consecutive movement must retain gait phase. A normal-speed in-game review must show clean starts, corners, stops and recovery with no visible skating, hovering, popping or detached equipment.

Supply the editable rig and motion, source pose curves or editable procedural pose rules, named clips, frame exposures, projected sole and hand anchors, exact contact/release/recovery markers and reproducible exports. Include the actual integrated 20–30-second sequence and a short professional assessment explaining any remaining acting issue. Dedicated terminal, mechanism, floor-item and suit/weapon handling corrections may be quoted as individually priced options when the benchmark demonstrates the need; do not silently bundle campaign-wide coverage.

Ask the proposal to price a blocking/contact review, one integrated motion review and two defined revision rounds, with explicit additional-round terms. Owner review and the experienced animator's assessment inform the final gate.

## Required quote terms

| Term | Proposal must state |
| --- | --- |
| Fixed price | Currency, taxes, payment milestones and optional work priced separately; currently TBD |
| Deliverables | Exact officer/loadout, room, mechanism, direction and clip coverage |
| Revisions | Included rounds, their scope, turnaround and extra-round price |
| Editable sources | File formats, rig/material dependencies and reproducible export instructions |
| Commercial use | Permission for game distribution, modification and derivative assets; third-party terms and attribution |
| Schedule | Availability, authoring/review/integration milestones and owner feedback assumptions |
| Acceptance | Running-game review at specified sizes/light states, defect correction and source handoff |

Campaign cost and schedule are calculated only after the accepted benchmark establishes actual authoring, correction and integration effort. Automated render and packing durations remain separate measurements.
