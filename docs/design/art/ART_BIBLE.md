# Origin War art bible / comparison production

Owner-approved production direction, 21 October 2026. Authority: systemic survival directive plus the art production plan. Status: production brief; comparison and owner artistic selection remain open.

## A vulnerable human inside an indifferent institution

An orbital station built to keep people alive continues performing its routines after those people have disappeared. The officer has a recognizable, fragile human body inside practical equipment. Rooms explain the work and authority that once occupied them. Tension grows from observation, uncertainty and purposeful machine behavior.

## Composition, camera and materials

Keep the square simulation grid with a three-quarter orthographic camera, approximately twelve cells vertically. Reference masters use 256 pixels per cell; the officer's standing silhouette is 1.3 cells high. Feet define actor position. Wide props retain their own bounds and physical footprints. The camera and scale must match between A/B candidates.

Ground is a quiet, low-contrast walking surface; structural depth belongs to ribbed bulkheads, seals, recesses, substantial doors and service runs. Control panels and workstations form purposeful groups. Leave deliberate negative space. Wear follows use: handles, boot routes, leak paths and interrupted maintenance. Repetition must communicate modular engineering without making every room interchangeable.

| Material / role | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Station structure | Graphite steel; broad readable planes, restrained edge wear |
| Officer shell / institutional fittings | Worn cream enamel; warm neutral highlights, darker mechanical joints |
| Flexible equipment | Charcoal rubber, woven harness and matte seals |
| Workplace surfaces | Mineral green, desaturated sage and blue-gray; construction varies by department |
| Status accents | Sparse amber and muted green tied to real functional state |
| Mars | Distant rust-colored relief only where a real window/view supports it |

Diffuse source painting must accept actual light. Separate powered emissions from diffuse highlights. Preserve observed darkness, remembered geometry and unseen space as distinct information. Smooth visible illumination cannot reveal concealed actors, remote power state or geometry. Cutaways protect the officer silhouette without disclosing hidden objects.

## Officer and motion

Retain the owner's pale sealed pressure suit, dark joints/harness, helmet/visor and mature human proportions. Keep gloves, soles, shell seams, pack and weapon grips consistent in every direction. Models and painted sheets share this design reference. Armor changes visibly attach to the same body.

Movement conveys load transfer and careful commitment: contact, compression, passing and lift. The head and torso retain believable mass. Hands remain attached to equipment; rifle grips use both hands. Start with twelve samples at 24fps over a two-foot cycle and a 60Hz display timeline. Calibrate stride to actual root travel; presentation duration may change while authoritative world ticks remain unchanged. Every step must stop cleanly at a planted pose. Avoid resetting the entire cycle at each tile.

Use small, deliberate ready/aim/release/recovery actions. Preserve sound marker order. Secondary straps and cables follow the authored action. Machines remain purposefully still when the world is waiting; no idle movement suggests an uncommitted tactical scan.

## Comparison delivery and decisions

Painted keyframes/in-betweens and rigged rendered sprites must share one room, camera, officer design, illumination and action timing. Both are candidates. Judge single/consecutive steps, turns, stops, aim/fire/recovery, long-gun grip, armor, pause/focus and changes in light at gameplay size. The implementation records unavailable states instead of silently presenting a wrong weapon or claiming complete coverage.

The first comparison is a deliberately staged production-engine art test, separately labeled from ordinary campaign play. The next gate is the approved 15–20-minute docking/maintenance slice. Owner artistic selection precedes campaign-wide asset expansion. Technical validation, captured performance, human readability and owner artistic acceptance remain separate claims.

## Sources, spending and reproducibility
