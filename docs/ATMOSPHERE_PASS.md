# Low-power station atmosphere pass

The owner's20October2026 brief is the creative authority: an institutional spacecraft in a low-power emergency, dark and foreboding with blinking consoles; abandoned-hospital unease; an immersive readable suit interface; smooth turn presentation and a pointed helmet light; heavy, enclosed vacuum acoustics with breath/heartbeat, restrained original organ music and frightened synthetic A.L.I.C.E. radio speech. The wall and floor textures require a full overhaul. There are no actual ghosts, borrowed franchise assets, new supernatural events or story changes.

## Implemented

- Full replacement of all five sector floor/wall families, glass, special movement/hazard floors, doors, consoles and institutional furniture. The original helmeted white/gray identity replaces the bareheaded player in walking, attack and collapse cells. Sixteen actual directional gait poses complement continuous movement.
- Separate SquidGrid `LightingManagerRgb`/`Radiance` fixture light against current bulkheads, a soft directional helmet beam, local peripheral wash, subtle power instability and visible indicator cores. The lamp aims with the mouse or walking direction and toggles with L. Reduced distortion removes fixture flicker. Lighting does not change legitimate vision, enemy awareness, turns or save data.
- SquidSmooth `VectorGlider` rendering of known cardinal motion, bounded actual corner waypoints, brief recoil/travel/explosion light, and physical split-panel doors. Unknown multi-tick enemy paths snap to their true endpoint rather than inventing a route through an obstacle. Input buffers at most one movement decision; pause/menus cancel it.
- A full-width station view with edge-mounted true suit telemetry, quiet task/log panels, smaller menu surfaces, smooth hover states and the existing readable scalable font. Full inventory, journal, map, record and attributes stay reachable through the suit menu/shortcuts. Recent transient messages fade; the expedition record retains recent log text. Authored dialogue remains fully readable and immediately selectable.
- Six original quiet organ-score roles; six continuous healthy/warning/critical breathing and heartbeat loops;34 low-pass/contact effects; nine actual A.L.I.C.E. speech clips through static and dry helmet/radio processing. Existing quoted words and complete text bindings are authoritative. No actor performance or human emotional acceptance is implied.
- Speech persists through pause/focus changes, is interrupted by actual dialogue changes, respects mute and never restarts per frame. Hidden events are silent; departing-floor combat cannot masquerade as new-floor audio. R.O.V.E.R. arming is tied to a real event and does not run a fake wall-clock countdown. The score ducks under speech/important cues.

The lead surveyed the creator's entire returned public repository catalog and relevant SquidSquad modules. The concrete runtime choices and primary source links are in [the ecosystem review](design/SQUID_ECOSYSTEM.md). Assets were developed independently by dedicated [art](design/art/ATM_ART_HANDOFF.md) and [sound](design/sound/ATM_SOUND_HANDOFF.md) workers, then integrated by the lead. Source rasters/prompts/native exports and deterministic composition/voice processing are retained. Free-use alternatives were researched; the selected world art is original generation and the selected published TTS model/data permissions are retained in the package's notices.

## Verification scopes

The original engine, campaign, finite-resource economy, seeded generation, save schema and story have no production-source changes in this pass. The larger new-expedition oxygen allocation from the prior feedback update remains intact. Existing saves retain their own resources.

New presentation tests cover occlusion/open-door rebuilding, directional falloff, strict hidden/unexplored masks, complete engine/RNG preservation, continuous retargeting, legitimate actor visibility, corner routing and cross-deck sound eligibility. Audio tests cover exact real-Campaign bindings, body thresholds, pause/mute/interruption/completion, bounded effect allocation and silence for hidden events. Static media validation fully decodes55 audio exports, checks source/license/output hashes, repeatability, loop diagnostics and conservative mix headroom; art validation checks all material replacements, exact nearest-neighbor output, pose anchors, changed cells and retained unrelated pixels. These checks have their own reports and do not establish listening or emotional effectiveness.

An initial real-window input check of the new presentation passed flashlight on/off, a single real movement turn, clicking Pack, Back/Escape, suit pause, saving to title and resuming. It used isolated test data and the presentation checkpoint before final asset integration; its transcript is kept distinct from final packaged acceptance. Source review corrected transition audio lineage and possible diagonal/corner interpolation. Final actual asset/framebuffer/animation/package results belong in the delivered companion verification receipt, with its exact source commit and archive SHA256.

Human headphone/speaker/mono listening, pronunciation/acting, full-campaign comfort, emotional unease and difficulty remain artistic/playtest judgments. The implementation can be inspected and played; no owner approval is inferred from automated validation.
