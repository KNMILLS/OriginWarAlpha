# Asset provenance

Representative M1 art is integrated from original built-in image generation. See [art manifest](art/manifest.json) for every source raster, slot, prompt, hash, export rule and limitation. The 8x8 atlas contains station tiles/items/effects and human/android/robot frame families, authored at native 32px and exported nearest-neighbor. Selected generation originals and the deterministic export source are retained. No legacy or third-party artwork was reused.

Real Windows framebuffer captures of gameplay and inventory were visually inspected at 1280x720, with native 32px art presented at 2x and an optional 1x zoom. This is representative M1 acceptance, not final multi-sector/directional/attack/death animation acceptance. Remaining production content and visual QA are M5 tasks.

Additional M5 content: 8x8 directional actor/action atlas, 8x4 sector floor/wall/prop atlas, and a2:1 ending-only illustration. Their source/prompt/slot/hash/validation records are under art/animation, art/sectors and art/finale, with source corrections/first failures retained. All are built-in OpenAI image_gen production from the original remake identity; no external packs/legacy art. Directional attack refinement and final runtime timing/seam/occlusion checks remain tracked independently.

Directed attacks add24 native32px frames in a separate8x3 atlas: player/S.I.R.S./A.D.D., north/east/south/west ready/recoil pairs. Six south cells preserve the accepted original actor pixels exactly; new illustrated directions retain prompts, first passes and compact corrections. tools/media/ATTACK_HANDOFF.md and art/animation/directional-actions/manifest.json identify all crops and hashes. The runtime now selects these from authoritative saved facing; static alpha/changed-pair/nearest4x/repacking checks PASS. Complete operator Windows runtime and independent combat/epilogue frame review are recorded in docs/VERIFICATION.md; they do not imply every attack/death variant was individually observed.

Stationary human NPC idles add an8x1 native32px atlas: two original illustrated poses each for West, Carmack and the survivor/medic, plus two exactly preserved operator frames. Sources, prompt, original-reference crops and repeatable export live under art/animation/humans and tools/media/HUMAN_HANDOFF.md. A final requirement review caught their previous static presentation; the new body/hand/head pairs complete the required human idle content. World rendering remains legitimate-FOV-only, and slow speaker avatars use the known current dialogue speaker without adding a station actor for offstage West. Animation uses presentation clock only; world turns, positions, flags and RNG streams are unchanged.

UI font: the first candidate used libGDX's small LSans15 bitmap. Owner feedback that all text was hard to read prompted OriginWarUI, a60px anti-aliased bitmap export of the exact Liberation Sans2.1.5 Regular TTF, rendered at a20px logical default with linear filtering independent of nearest-filtered world art. The retained source is assets/ui/source/LiberationSans-Regular.ttf; source SHA25676d04c18ea243f426b7de1f3ad208e927008f961dc5945e5aad352d0dfde8ee8. tools/media/build_ui_font.py and assets/ui/manifest.json record source/export hashes,199glyphs and the official upstream archive. SIL OFL1.1, Google2010 / Red Hat2012; complete grant remains licenses/font/LICENSE-OFL-1.1. The renamed bitmap derivative is not covered solely by libGDX's Apache license. Matthias Mann's separate BSD BitmapFont notice remains preserved in licenses/libgdx/SOURCE_HEADERS.txt.

Owner clarification: they authored the original art/sound design, with unease and tension as the intended feeling. That is creative authority; generic legacy-rights language does not dismiss their own illustration. The current remake still uses its separately produced assets. The original helmeted illustration, prototype grammar and emotional continuity are explicitly audited in docs/design/art, with proposed bounded comparisons. Rights/attribution for bundled third-party fonts and any individual sourced legacy recordings remain separate asset-level questions; no legacy recording was imported by this feedback update.

ATM-ART is the later owner-authorized implementation of the institutional station/helmet direction, superseding the earlier material/player cells described above. New original built-in image_gen sources replace all actual ordinary/special floor, wall and glass texture paths across five zones, plus institutional props/doors and every player walk/attack/death cell. Existing128px runtime atlas interfaces remain stable; the current mixed pack uses native64 at nearest2x while untouched previous cells remain exact runtime pixels. A new optional4x4 player gait atlas adds four physical poses per facing. The owner's enclosed white/gray helmet portrait is retained as an identity reference copy under art/atmosphere/source; its original legacy file is unchanged and its rights are not relabeled as a generic third-party grant. No externally sourced pack, franchise imagery, supernatural artwork or recording was imported. Primary CC0 alternatives were researched but not used.

The ATM manifest, retained generator sources/first failures, prompt records, output hashes, deterministic art-only packer and static validation are under art/atmosphere and tools/media. assets/art/.gitattributes preserves exact source/export/manifest bytes through checkouts. docs/design/art/ATM_ART_HANDOFF.md records exact replaced slots, renderer methods/core coordinates, primary research links, independent exported contact/tiling inspection and remaining actual gameplay/lighting/motion/owner-acceptance limits. Prior pass manifests and validations are historical evidence; ATM manifest/validation identify the current overridden cells.
